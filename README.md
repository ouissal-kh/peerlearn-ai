# PeerLearn AI

> AI Operating System for Collaborative Learning

PeerLearn AI is an AI-powered platform that transforms collaborative learning into a structured, personalized, and engaging experience.

Instead of simply answering questions, PeerLearn AI helps learners:

- Build personalized learning roadmaps
- Form effective study groups
- Organize collaborative study sessions
- Evaluate learning progress
- Stay motivated through AI guidance

---

## Vision

Empower every learner with AI-driven collaborative learning.

---

## Problem

Millions of learners struggle with:

- Information overload
- Lack of learning direction
- Studying alone
- Low motivation
- Inconsistent progress

Existing AI tools answer questions.

PeerLearn AI helps learners **learn together**.

---

## MVP Features

- AI Learning Planner
- Team Formation
- AI Study Sessions
- Progress Dashboard
- AI Evaluation
- Learning Roadmaps
